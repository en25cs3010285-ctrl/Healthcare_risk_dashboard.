2. Data Visualization Guide (Tableau)

Project: Health360, Comprehensive Healthcare Risk & Disease Analytics

Calculated fields

// Age Group
IF [Age] < 18 THEN "0-17"
ELSEIF [Age] < 30 THEN "18-29"
ELSEIF [Age] < 45 THEN "30-44"
ELSEIF [Age] < 60 THEN "45-59"
ELSE "60+" END

// BMI Category
IF [BMI] < 18.5 THEN "Underweight"
ELSEIF [BMI] < 25 THEN "Normal"
ELSEIF [BMI] < 30 THEN "Overweight"
ELSE "Obese" END

// Any Disease
IF [Heart Disease]=1 OR [Diabetes]=1 OR [Stroke]=1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END

// Risk Score
(IF [Smoking]=1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
+ (IF [Alcohol]=1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
+ (IF [BMI]>=30 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
+ (IF [Cholesterol]>=240 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
+ (IF [Glucose]>=126 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
+ (IF [Blood Pressure]>=140 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)

// Risk Level
IF [Risk Score]>=4 THEN "High"
ELSEIF [Risk Score]>=2 THEN "Medium"
ELSE "Low" END

Measures

Total Patients = COUNTD([Patient ID])
Diseased Patients = SUM([Any Disease])
Prevalence % = SUM([Any Disease]) / COUNTD([Patient ID])

Format Prevalence % as a percentage with 1 decimal. Adjust the 1/0 conditions to Yes/No if the dataset uses text values.

4. Case Study: Early Detection & Risk Intelligence for Chronic Diseases

Executive summary

Chronic conditions such as heart disease, diabetes and stroke pose severe challenges to healthcare systems. Delayed diagnosis amplifies patient complications and inflates medical expenditure. This project delivers a Tableau health analytics suite that links demographic data, clinical vital signs and behavioral indicators, helping clinical teams and public health leaders move from reactive treatment to proactive risk prevention.

The problem

Healthcare systems capture large volumes of clinical and lifestyle data, but key indicators, from blood pressure trends and fasting glucose to exercise habits, often sit in disconnected spreadsheets. Manual analysis makes it hard for care managers to spot multi-variable risk patterns early, which delays interventions and leads to preventable emergency admissions.

Persona-driven scenarios

Dr. Michael Carter

Clinical Risk Assessment Officer

Challenge: Hard to spot high-risk patients quickly because records are processed manually.

Solution: Tableau early-warning scorecards and scatter plots combining BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol and glucose.

Outcome: Critical cohorts are identified fast for immediate follow-up, reducing complications.

Emily Rodriguez

Healthcare Data Analyst

Challenge: Understanding how lifestyle choices compound health risks across different patient groups.

Solution: Correlation matrices and lifestyle heatmaps linking exercise, smoking and alcohol to disease prevalence.

Outcome: Data-backed evidence for targeted preventive care plans.

Dr. Priya Nair

Public Health Researcher

Challenge: Analyzing population-wide trends and vulnerability without clear visual aggregation.

Solution: Age-group distributions, BMI classifications and symptom frequency maps (fatigue, chest pain, dizziness).

Outcome: Guides allocation of public health resources, screening drives and preventive policy.

5. Final Project Report: Comprehensive Healthcare Risk & Disease Analysis

Data Analytics with Tableau (Skillwallet / FutureSkills). Submitted by Nandita Telang, B.Tech CSE (2nd Year), Medicaps University, Indore.

1. Introduction

Overview. Health360 is a Tableau project that studies patient demographics, clinical indicators and lifestyle behaviours to find disease trends and health risk correlations. The dataset covers age, BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, smoking, alcohol, fatigue, chest pain, dizziness and exercise level, along with heart disease, diabetes and stroke.

The project follows seven milestones: Data Collection & Extraction, Data Preparation, Data Visualization, Dashboard, Story, Performance Testing and Web Integration. The output is a set of interactive dashboards and a data story that help healthcare professionals detect high-risk individuals, monitor health trends and plan preventive care.

Purpose.

  • Identify high-risk patients early using clinical indicators.
  • Understand how smoking, alcohol and exercise influence chronic disease.
  • Analyse disease prevalence across age groups and BMI categories to find vulnerable segments.
  • Give visual insights that support clinical decisions, preventive care and resource allocation.

2. Ideation phase

Problem statement. Chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes and stroke make early identification of high-risk patients a major challenge. Manual record analysis is slow and hides patterns, and late detection brings complications, higher costs and poorer outcomes. An interactive visual way to analyse health indicators and lifestyle factors together is needed.

4. Project design

Problem-solution fit. Clinicians and analysts cannot quickly see which patients and groups are at high risk. Tableau dashboards turn large patient records into interactive visuals, and each persona is served directly:

  • Dr. Carter gets KPI metrics and clinical indicator charts to flag high-risk individuals.
  • Emily Rodriguez gets heatmaps and comparative charts to link lifestyle with disease.
  • Dr. Priya Nair gets age-group, BMI and symptom views to find vulnerable populations.

Proposed solution. A set of interactive Tableau dashboards:

  • Overview dashboard: KPI cards and overall disease prevalence.
  • Clinical indicators dashboard: BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol and glucose.
  • Lifestyle dashboard: smoking, alcohol and exercise against heart disease, diabetes and stroke.
  • Demographic dashboard: prevalence by age group and BMI category.
  • Symptoms view: chest pain, fatigue and dizziness patterns.
  • Story: a guided narrative of key findings and recommendations.

Solution architecture. Four layers: the data layer (raw dataset), the preparation layer (cleaning and calculated fields), the analytics layer (worksheets, filters, parameters, KPIs) and the presentation layer (dashboards, story, web integration).

8. Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • Turns large patient datasets into clear, interactive visuals.
  • Quickly identifies high-risk individuals and vulnerable groups.
  • Reveals links between lifestyle behaviours and chronic disease.
  • Supports preventive care, treatment planning and resource allocation.
  • Easy to share on the web, with no technical skill needed.

Disadvantages

  • Accuracy depends on the quality and completeness of the dataset.
  • Shows correlations, not proven causes, so findings need clinical validation.
  • Does not predict individual risk with a machine learning model.
  • Static dataset, so no real-time updates without a live source.
  • Patient data needs careful privacy handling when published.

9. Conclusion

Health360 shows how Tableau can convert complex healthcare data into clear, actionable insight. By analysing clinical indicators, lifestyle behaviours and demographics together, the dashboards help clinicians identify high-risk patients, help analysts understand lifestyle influences and help researchers find vulnerable populations. The project supports proactive healthcare management, better preventive strategies and more informed clinical and policy decisions.
